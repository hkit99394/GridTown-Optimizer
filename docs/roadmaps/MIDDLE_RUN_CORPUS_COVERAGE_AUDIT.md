## Promotion Use

- Broad `auto` default-path candidates may use the current product workflow matrix as the protected development/holdout base, but reviewers must call out single-split family concentration when it appears.
- Family-targeted promotion claims need both a development-side signal and a protected/fresh holdout signal for the affected family; diagnostics-only probes may stop at a narrower slice if the intake says so.
- Learned-LNS fresh-pressure holdout artifacts are useful track-specific evidence, but they do not replace a fresh product-workflow cross-mode holdout.
- The first product-workflow fresh holdout cases are now implemented. The first CP-SAT intake carried candidate-specific fresh-holdout evaluator validity through L0/M9 and then closed diagnostics-only; future candidates must repeat that gate for their own affected modes and slices.
